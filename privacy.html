WantedClipzTV – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

Short version

This tool is only used for my own TikTok account. It doesn't collect anyone else's data, doesn't run ads, and doesn't sell or share anything.

What it uses from TikTok

When I connect my TikTok account, TikTok gives the tool my basic account info and a login key so it can send my approved clips to my drafts. That's all it's used for.

Where it's stored

Everything stays on my own computer. Nothing is sent anywhere except to TikTok.

About the clips

The clips feature footage owned by the original creators. We don't own it and we credit them. Creators can ask for any clip to be removed.

Removing access

The connection can be removed at any time in TikTok's settings.
